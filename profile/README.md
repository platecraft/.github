# PlateCraft

**PlateCraft** is building a smarter recipe, meal planning, nutrition, and household food management platform.

Our goal is to help people plan what to eat, understand how recipes fit their goals, organize shopping, manage inventory, and coordinate cooking in a way that feels practical for real households.

## What PlateCraft is building

PlateCraft is designed around a few core ideas:

- Recipes should understand nutrition, portions, preferences, and constraints.
- Meal planning should adapt to real schedules, households, events, guests, and goals.
- Grocery lists should connect planning, shopping, inventory, and consumption.
- Kitchens, pantries, equipment, and storage should work together as a household food system.
- AI should assist, review, adapt, and explain — not replace deterministic planning logic.

## Core repositories

- `recipe-meal-planner` — PlateCraft app development.
- `platecraft-landing` — Public marketing site and external-facing pages.

## Project direction

PlateCraft is being developed as a modular platform with first-class systems for:

- Identity and household ownership
- Recipes and ingredients
- Measurement and nutrition calculation
- Meal planning and schedules
- Inventory and kitchen management
- Shopping and consumption workflows
- Events, guests, vacations, holidays, and reminders
- AI-assisted validation, planning, and adaptation
